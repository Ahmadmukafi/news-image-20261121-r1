One image summing up the world news of Saturday 21 November 2026, drawn as a
broadsheet front page. Round 1 leads with Russia's biggest barrage of the war
— 400+ drones and 110+ missiles on Ukraine overnight, three Dnipro bridges
hit, at least eight killed, and Russian missiles coming down in NATO Poland —
plus the AI sell-off that pushed the S&P 500 into correction, the UN court's
genocide ruling against Myanmar, COP31 running past its deadline, a pancreatic
cancer pill breakthrough, UK–EU post-Brexit deals and GTA 6's record day one.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the pre-rendered `entry.png`
to the output directory. `render.py` is the Pillow script that drew the page.
